1. **Dual-Coding Balance**: Animations default to graphics, geometric metaphors, and icons, utilizing minimal text and reserving exact on-screen wording exclusively for definitions, terminal commands, or essential numeric labels.

3. **5-to-8s Micro-Reset Loop**: Avoid saccadic habituation by executing purposeful spatial camera movements ($2\times$ cursor-dwell zoom, 3D canvas shift, or dashboard whip-pan) at regular 5–8 second intervals.
4. **Held Final Frame Rule (System 2 Encoding)**: Every high-velocity data sequence or chart must hold its final frame statically for $1.5\text{ to }2.0\text{ seconds}$ before cutting to allow prefrontal cognitive consolidation.
